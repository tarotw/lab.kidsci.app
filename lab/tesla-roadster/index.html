  • 吸地風扇：把車子底下的空氣抽走，大氣把車往下壓，輪胎抓得更牢。
  • 冷氣推進器：噴出高壓空氣，反作用力直接推車，不需要靠輪胎。
  • 功率與齒輪：起步靠扭力和抓地力，高速靠功率；齒輪比決定極速。
  • Suction fans: pull air from under the car so the atmosphere presses it down and the tires grip harder.
  • Cold-gas thrusters: blast compressed air; the reaction pushes the car without using the tires.
  • Power and gearing: launch depends on torque and grip; speed depends on power, and gearing sets the top speed.

      模型怎麼算？

      每 1/480 秒計算一次牛頓第二定律。後輪能推多大力，取三者中最小的：輪胎抓地力、馬達扭力、馬達功率 ÷ 速度；馬達轉到 23,500 rpm 時由齒輪比決定極速。推進器的推力直接加上去，不經過輪胎，直到氣瓶用完。

      m·a = F後輪 + F推進器 − F風阻 − F滾阻
      F後輪 ≤ μ ×（後輪負重 + 風扇下壓力的後輪部分 + 加速時的重量轉移）
      F後輪 ≤ 扭力 × 齒輪比 ÷ 輪半徑 ・ F後輪 ≤ 功率 ÷ 速度
      噴氣時間 ≈ 可用空氣質量 ÷（推力 ÷ 650 m/s）

      推估值：車身（不含電池）1,080 kg、駕駛 75 kg、電池每 kWh 6 kg、氣瓶每公升 0.6 kg 外加 0.55 kg 的高壓空氣（約 690 bar）、可用空氣 85%、噴氣速度 650 m/s、後輪靜態負重 56%、輪半徑 0.355 m、迎風面積 1.75 m²、滾動阻力係數 0.013。續航以 110 km/h 定速計算。風扇耗電以每 4,700 kgf 約 80 kW 估算。這些數字讓三個版本接近官方 0–60 mph 宣稱，但都不是特斯拉公布的資料。

      發表會的兩個特技

      貼牆坡道：車子沿著像半個雲霄飛車迴圈的弧形坡道往上爬，頂端約 21 公尺、幾乎倒過來（約 170°）。特斯拉說底盤風扇最多能產生約 2 g 的吸力，讓車倒著也貼得住；接著冷氣推進器把車推離坡道，前方推進器讓車身沿著車長方向左右翻轉 180°，車子翻正後車頭朝後（朝助跑來的方向）落地。本實驗用「自動駕駛定速」爬坡，檢查三件事：坡道推輪子的力 N = m·v²/R + 風扇吸力 + m·g·cosθ 是否一直大於 0、後輪抓地力 μ×N 是否推得贏重力，以及推進器的空氣夠不夠翻正和剎車。

      飛越車隊：車子飛過並排停放的 Cybercab、Model 3、Model Y、Cybertruck 和 4.1 公尺高的 Semi，最高約 7–8 公尺，還在空中桶滾一圈。本實驗用車底噴嘴朝下噴氣起飛，之後是拋體運動；車子的外形會跟每台車的高度比對，桶滾時車身變「高」會吃掉間距。車輛排列順序、間距、坡道寬度與噴嘴配置都是示意，並非發表會實際尺寸。

      噴流裡的「馬赫環」

      高壓空氣從噴嘴衝出時比音速還快，壓力又比外面的空氣高，噴流會一下膨脹、一下被壓回去，形成一串菱形的震波，叫做「馬赫環」（shock diamonds）。第一個馬赫環離噴嘴約 x ≈ 0.67 × 噴嘴直徑 × √(噴嘴壓力 ÷ 大氣壓)。本實驗假設每個噴嘴直徑 4 cm、推力係數 1.5，用推力反推噴嘴壓力：推力越大，壓力越高，馬赫環越拉開。注意：冷空氣不會像火箭火焰那樣發光，現實中要靠水氣凝結或紋影攝影才看得見；發表會報導也沒有提到。畫面上的亮點是為了教學而標出位置。

      小朋友的表情代表什麼？

      香氛吊飾的角度就是「加速度 ÷ 重力」的反正切，和真實車上吊飾的物理一樣。推背感的 g 值是模型算出的水平加速度除以 9.81；表情只是比喻。太空梭升空約 3 g、一般人在雲霄飛車上約 1–1.5 g 水平加速度。

      How the model works

      Newton's second law, every 1/480 s. The rear tires push with the smallest of tire grip, motor torque and motor power ÷ speed; at 23,500 rpm the gear ratio caps the top speed. Thruster force is added directly, bypassing the tires, until the tank is empty.

      m·a = Frear + Fthrust − Fdrag − Frolling
      Frear ≤ μ × (rear load + rear share of fan downforce + weight transfer)
      Frear ≤ torque × gear ÷ wheel radius · Frear ≤ power ÷ speed
      burn time ≈ usable air mass ÷ (thrust ÷ 650 m/s)

      Estimates: body without battery 1,080 kg, driver 75 kg, 6 kg per kWh, tank 0.6 kg per litre plus 0.55 kg of air at ≈690 bar, 85% usable air, 650 m/s exhaust, 56% static rear load, 0.355 m wheel radius, 1.75 m² frontal area, rolling coefficient 0.013. Range is a steady 110 km/h cruise. Fan power is ≈80 kW per 4,700 kgf. These values bring the three versions close to Tesla's 0–60 claims, but none of them are Tesla data.

      The two reveal stunts

      Wall ramp: the car climbed a curved ramp shaped like half a roller-coaster loop to about 21 m, nearly upside down (about 170°). Tesla says the underbody fans can pull up to about 2 g, so the car sticks even inverted; cold-gas thrusters then pushed it off and front thrusters rolled it 180° about its length, so it came down upright facing backwards, toward where it started. This lab climbs at a constant autopilot speed and checks three things: whether the ramp keeps pressing on the wheels, N = m·v²/R + fan suction + m·g·cosθ > 0; whether rear-tire grip μ×N can beat gravity; and whether the air lasts for the flip and the braking burn.

      Fleet jump: the car leapt over a parked Cybercab, Model 3, Model Y, Cybertruck and the 4.1 m Semi, peaking around 7–8 m, with a barrel roll. Here the down nozzles lift the car, then it follows projectile motion; the car’s outline is checked against each vehicle’s height, and rolling makes the car taller and eats into the gap. Vehicle order, spacing, ramp width and nozzle layout are illustrative, not the reveal’s exact dimensions.

      Mach diamonds in the jet

      Compressed air leaves the nozzle faster than sound and at a higher pressure than the air around it, so the jet expands, gets squeezed back, and repeats, forming a chain of diamond-shaped shock waves. The first one sits about x ≈ 0.67 × nozzle diameter × √(nozzle pressure ÷ atmospheric pressure) from the nozzle. This lab assumes 4 cm nozzles and a thrust coefficient of 1.5 and works the nozzle pressure out from the thrust: more thrust, higher pressure, wider spacing. Cold air does not glow like a rocket flame; in real life you only see the diamonds through condensation or schlieren photography, and the reveal coverage does not mention them. The bright beads mark where they would be.

      What do the faces mean?

      The air freshener hangs at arctan(acceleration ÷ gravity), exactly like in a real car. The g number is the model's forward acceleration ÷ 9.81; the faces are just a metaphor. A Space Shuttle launch peaks around 3 g.
